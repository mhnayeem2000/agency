{# ---------------- FAQ ---------------- #}

Helpful answers

Good questions deserve clear answers.

Still unsure about something? Message us and a counsellor will reply within one working day.

Ask a counsellor →
What can Global EduLink help me with?

Course and university selection, application preparation, document planning, scholarship guidance, offer comparison and visa interview preparation — all tracked inside your student account.

How much does the service cost?

Your first profile assessment is free. Service charges depend on the destination and number of universities, and they are documented in writing before you commit to anything.

Do I need IELTS before contacting you?

No. Many universities accept a conditional offer while you prepare your English test. We plan the timeline so your test date never delays the application.

Can you help with scholarships?

Yes. We map merit and need-based scholarships you qualify for, prepare the supporting documents and track each deadline for you.

How long does the whole process take?

Most students go from first consultation to visa decision in four to seven months, depending on the intake and how quickly documents are completed.

How do I track my application?

Every registered student gets a dashboard with live status, document checklist, timeline and notifications from the agency team.
